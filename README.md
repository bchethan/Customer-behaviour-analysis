# Customer Shopping Behaviour Analysis

## Overview

This project analyzes customer shopping behaviour to identify purchasing patterns, customer segments, product preferences, discount usage, subscription behaviour, and spending trends.

The objective is to understand what influences customer purchasing decisions and provide actionable recommendations to improve customer engagement, loyalty, and marketing strategies.

The project follows an end-to-end data analytics workflow using Python, MySQL, and Power BI, supported by a business report and presentation created using Gamma.

## Business Problem

How can a retail company leverage customer shopping data to identify trends, improve customer engagement, and optimize marketing and product strategies?

The analysis focuses on:
- Understanding customer demographics and spending patterns.
- Identifying loyal, returning, and new customers.
- Evaluating subscription behaviour and discount usage.
- Comparing product ratings and shipping preferences.
- Identifying opportunities to improve marketing and customer retention.

## Dataset

The dataset contains **3,900 customer purchase records and 18 columns** covering customer demographics, product information, transaction details, and shopping preferences.

Key attributes include:

| Category | Features |
|---|---|
| Customer Demographics | Age, Gender, Location |
| Purchase Details | Item Purchased, Category, Purchase Amount |
| Shopping Behaviour | Previous Purchases, Frequency of Purchases |
| Promotions | Discount Applied, Promo Code Used |
| Customer Experience | Review Rating, Shipping Type |
| Preferences | Subscription Status, Payment Method, Season, Size, Color |

**Data preparation:** 37 missing review ratings were handled using the median rating of the corresponding product category. Column names were standardized, and additional features were created for age groups and purchase frequency.

## Project Workflow

### 1. Data Loading and Exploration — Python

- Loaded the raw dataset using Pandas.
- Examined dataset dimensions, data types, and summary statistics.
- Identified missing values and reviewed data consistency.
- Explored customer demographics, purchase amounts, product categories, and shopping preferences.

### 2. Data Cleaning and Feature Engineering — Python

- Handled missing review ratings using category-level median imputation.
- Standardized column names to snake_case.
- Created an `age_group` feature to categorize customers by age.
- Created a `purchase_frequency_days` feature.
- Examined overlapping information between discount and promo code columns and removed `promo_code_used` from the prepared dataset.
- Loaded the cleaned data into MySQL for further analysis.

### 3. SQL Analysis — MySQL

Executed SQL queries to answer business questions, including:

- What is the total revenue generated by male and female customers?
- Which customers used discounts but spent above the average purchase amount?
- Which products have the highest average review ratings?
- How do average purchase amounts compare between standard and express shipping?
- How do subscribers and non-subscribers differ in average spending and total revenue?
- Which products have the highest discount rates?
- How are customers distributed across new, returning, and loyal segments?
- What are the most purchased products within each category?
- Are frequent buyers more likely to subscribe?
- Which age groups contribute the most revenue?

SQL concepts used include filtering, aggregation, `GROUP BY`, subqueries, conditional expressions, Common Table Expressions (CTEs), and window functions.

### 4. Dashboard Development — Power BI

Built an interactive Customer Behaviour Dashboard to communicate key metrics and trends.

The dashboard includes:
- Total customers
- Average purchase amount
- Average review rating
- Subscription distribution
- Revenue by product category
- Revenue and sales by age group
- Filters for subscription status, gender, category, and shipping type

The dashboard helps users explore customer behaviour and compare performance across different segments.

### 5. Reporting and Presentation

- Prepared a project report documenting the dataset, data preparation, SQL analysis, findings, and recommendations.
- Created an executive-style presentation using Gamma to communicate the key insights and proposed business actions.

## Key Findings

### Customer Segmentation

- **3,116 loyal customers**
- **701 returning customers**
- **83 new customers**

The customer distribution indicates an opportunity to strengthen retention and encourage more customers to make repeat purchases.

### Revenue by Gender

- Male customers generated **157,890** in total purchase revenue.
- Female customers generated **75,191** in total purchase revenue.

Male customers contributed a larger share of the recorded purchase revenue in this dataset.

### Subscribers vs. Non-Subscribers

| Metric | Subscribers | Non-Subscribers |
|---|---:|---:|
| Customers | 1,053 | 2,847 |
| Average Spend | 59.49 | 59.87 |
| Total Revenue | 62,645 | 170,436 |

Non-subscribers generated more total revenue because they represented a larger customer group. Average spending was slightly higher among non-subscribers, so subscription status alone did not indicate higher spending.

### Shipping Type

| Shipping Type | Average Purchase Amount |
|---|---:|
| Express | 60.48 |
| Standard | 58.46 |

Express-shipping purchases had a higher average purchase amount, suggesting an opportunity to test premium shipping offers and related promotions.

### Product Ratings and Discounts

- Sandals recorded the highest average review rating at **3.82**.
- Boots followed at **3.79**.
- Hats had the highest discount rate at **50.00%**, followed by sneakers at **49.66%** and coats at **49.07%**.

These findings can help distinguish products that perform well on customer ratings from those frequently purchased with discounts.

### Revenue by Age Group

- Young Adults: **62,143**
- Middle-aged: **59,197**
- Adults: **59,978**
- Seniors: **55,763**

Young Adults were the largest revenue-contributing age group in the analysis.

*Note: Revenue figures and spending amounts reflect the values recorded in the dataset.*

## Business Recommendations

1. **Improve customer loyalty:** Introduce targeted rewards and retention campaigns to encourage repeat purchases.
2. **Increase subscription adoption:** Test exclusive benefits and offers for frequent buyers without assuming that subscriptions automatically increase spending.
3. **Optimize discount strategies:** Review discount-heavy products to balance purchase volume and potential profit margins.
4. **Promote high-rated products:** Feature well-rated and popular products in category-specific marketing campaigns.
5. **Test shipping-based offers:** Evaluate premium shipping promotions to determine whether the higher average purchase amount translates into better business outcomes.
6. **Improve audience targeting:** Use age group, purchase history, product preferences, and subscription status together to design targeted campaigns.
